🌿 Who We Are

Alhaja Shyfa is a certified Natural Health Practitioner from Osun State, Nigeria, with over 30 years of experience in traditional and natural wellness. Her knowledge of herbs, roots, and natural remedies was inherited from generations of family healing practice. What gives her the greatest joy is the positive feedback from patients—especially TTC mothers who conceived within three months of using her remedies. Seeing lives transformed and families blessed through natural healing remains her greatest fulfillment. Alhaja Shyfa is committed to helping people restore balance and health naturally, using remedies passed down through generations and refined with modern safety standards.

🏅 Certification & Trust

Alhaja Shyfa is a licensed member of the National Traditional Medicine Practitioners (NTMP). She is also a member of the Elewe Omo Herbal Practitioners, Lagos State, and operates under strict professional and ethical standards.
